Privacy Policy

The short version: no ads, no tracking cookies, and nothing about you gets sold.

Reading the site

This site is a set of plain web pages. Reading it doesn't need an account, and doesn't set any cookies.

The pages are delivered through a content delivery network, which keeps standard server logs (things like your IP address, browser and the page requested) for a short time, for security and performance.

Fonts are your device's own, and images come from this site (the one exception is the live stream preview described below). The only other places a page talks to are the analytics, comments and stream check described below.

Analytics

I use Fathom Analytics to see how many people visit and which pages they read. Fathom doesn't use cookies and doesn't collect personal information, so there's no way for it, or me, to identify you or follow you around the web.

Comments

Comments are run on my own server with Comentario, not a third-party service. If you sign in with Apple, Google, Facebook or GitHub to leave a comment, that service shares your name, email address and profile picture with it. Your name, picture and comments are shown publicly, and your email address never is.

You can also make an account with just an email address and password. Comentario stores your email, the name you choose and a hashed copy of your password (never the password itself). Or you can comment without an account at all, in which case only the name you type in (or “Anonymous”) is kept, and shown with your comment.

Signing in to comment stores your login session in a cookie on tim.dev (“comentario_commenter_session”), and signing out removes it. If you don't sign in, no cookie is set. Comment data is only used to run the comments, and never for marketing.

Posts load their comments, and the home page its comment counts, from comments.tim.dev, and Comentario keeps basic page view statistics. Any IP addresses it records, with comments or views, are stored in shortened form only.

If you'd like your comments or account removed, get in touch and I'll take care of it.

Videos and streams

YouTube and Twitch players stay as plain placeholders until you press play. Only then do they load from YouTube (using its privacy-enhanced mode) or Twitch, and their own privacy policies apply from that point.

On the home page, the site checks with a small server of my own whether I'm streaming on Twitch right now. That check only returns my stream status. While I'm live, the home page also shows the stream's preview image, which loads from Twitch.

Stored on your device

The site remembers your light or dark theme choice in your browser's local storage. On post pages, the comment box also saves your unfinished draft there, so it isn't lost if you reload, and remembers your comment settings, such as the name you used if you commented without an account. None of it is sent anywhere, except the name and text of a comment you post.

Email

If you email me, I'll only use your message and address to reply.

Questions

If you have any questions about any of this, please get in touch. I'll update this page if anything changes.
