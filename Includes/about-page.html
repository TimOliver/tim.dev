I'm originally from Perth: a beautiful, peaceful little city on Australia's west coast.

My parents have always been Apple fans, so there's always been a Mac in the house. Tinkering around on the family Mac with graphics, animation, web development and game mods is what sparked my interest in digital creativity.

That interest became a university degree in Digital Media, which taught me equal parts computer science, multimedia and graphic design. That led to my first job as a frontend web developer, turning the designers’ mockups into working HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Flash.

Then, everything changed when the iPhone 3G landed in Australia. I was absolutely enamoured with the idea of an always-online computer in my pocket. I bought an introductory book, went to every iOS meetup in my area, and started shipping apps to the App Store. In 2013, I officially switched to full-time iOS development for good.

What started as some small side projects has opened doors to great companies in the US and Japan. In 2016 I moved to San Francisco to work at a startup in the Bay Area and experience Silicon Valley firsthand. And in 2018, work brought me to Tokyo, which I've called home ever since.

In more recent years, I had the privilege of working at Instagram in Tokyo, directly contributing to the launch of Threads and Instagram for iPad, shipping code to hundreds of millions of Apple devices. It's been the absolute ride of my life.

These days I'm at a brand-new startup in Tokyo, still shipping my own apps and libraries on the side. Technology is moving faster today than at any point I've seen in my career, and I can't wait to see what it lets me build next.

Nice to meet you!

About this site

[tagline placeholder] Built with Ignite, designed with Claude, served as flat files.

For most of its life, tim.dev ran on WordPress - a setup that served me well for the better part of a decade, but had quietly grown into more maintenance than writing. This version is a clean rebuild: a fully static site generated by Ignite, the Swift-based generator by Paul Hudson.

The visual language - the type, the palette, the rhythm of the pages - is mine, decided the old-fashioned way over a lot of evenings. The build itself owes an honest amount to Claude Design and Claude Code, which I leaned on to translate the design into a working site. A genuine collaboration, and a fast one.

No database, no admin panel, no plugins to keep up to date - just files, hosted on GitHub Pages, served straight from the repo.
