SLKMeter was an app I helped build for Fulton Hogan while working at Lateral. Road maintenance in Western Australia tracks road lengths and positions in 'straight-line kilometres', a specialised measurement that has to be calculated in software.

Fulton Hogan commissioned Lateral to build SLKMeter for both iOS and Android, so we chose Xamarin to build a single cross-platform app.

This app was a lot of firsts for me: my first Android app, and my first app in Xamarin. I learned a lot, and that Xamarin experience came in handy later with osu!
