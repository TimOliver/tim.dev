I'll maintain forever that nothing else in my career will top this. Being part of the team at IG that finally brought the app to iPad was the pleasure and privilege of a lifetime.

It was a fascinating challenge. We were working in a codebase well over a decade old, where no one had ever thought about future-proofing for iPad. It was a very methodical process: auditing every screen, building as much reusable iPad layout code as possible, and adding automatic fallbacks for the screens we hadn't reached yet.

I focused on two big things: a reusable engine that gave all the major screens fluid rotation animations, and making keyboard and mouse support on iPadOS a first-class citizen.
