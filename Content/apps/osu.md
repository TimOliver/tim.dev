osu! is a rhythm game for all the major platforms. Inspired by games like Elite Beat Agents, it has you tapping circles on screen in time to a song. Simple in theory, but the skill curve is absolutely insane.

The game was built from the ground up to be cross-platform. It's already out on Windows and Mac, with iPhone and iPad releases coming soon.

I help out with the Apple platform side of the osu! engine from time to time, with things like iPad Magic Keyboard support and updating the app icon for Liquid Glass.
