Instagram rarely launches whole new apps, so the launch of Threads was incredibly exciting.

My team in Japan worked with the core Threads team to get everything ready for launch. My manager and I helped build the hype by hiding an Easter egg in the Instagram app: the Golden Ticket.

The launch went on to be one of the biggest success stories in recent App Store history.

Afterwards, my team worked on all sorts of features for Threads. The ones I contributed to included Voice Threads, hiding the IG badge on profiles, a refined photo and video viewer, and photo tagging.
