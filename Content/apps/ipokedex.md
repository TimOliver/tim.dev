iPokédex was a fully comprehensive Pokédex app, with complete data on the first five generations of the main Pokémon games: every Pokémon (with their moves, evolutions and stats), every move, and every ability.

I decided to build it in 2010 while playing the DS games, when looking up walkthroughs online was painfully slow. I was also playing in Japanese, so I figured a bilingual Pokémon database that worked offline would be really handy.

Turns out it was! Since it had all of the Japanese names and data, it absolutely took off in Japan, eventually reaching number 7 in the overall free app rankings on the Japanese iTunes Store.

Unfortunately, it was not to be. With that much visibility, I was contacted by Pokémon's general counsel, who thanked me for being a fan, but asked me to take it down.

It was an amazing ride, and I'm glad I had the experience. Taking it down cleared the way for me to start building iComics, so all things considered, it was for the best.
