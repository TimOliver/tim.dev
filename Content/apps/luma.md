Having been to so many conferences and meetups over the years, I've tried more than my fair share of event platforms.

Luma started during the pandemic as a way to run virtual meetups, and kept evolving from there. Fast forward to today, and it's absolutely popped off around the world, especially in Tokyo! It's a beautifully designed app, built on Apple's latest frameworks.

I met the Luma crew back in 2021, when they'd adopted my image cropping library and needed a little help integrating it. I've been helping them out with new features whenever I get the chance ever since!
