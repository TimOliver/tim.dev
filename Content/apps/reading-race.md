Reading Race was a series of apps I helped build at Lateral for our client, PLD (Promoting Literacy Development).

They were multiplayer games that taught young kids English pronunciation. The iPad sat between two kids, and each of them got the same options on their own edge of the screen.

We built a single engine that let us reuse the codebase across four apps, each with the same structure and behaviour but different graphics and content.

It was a really fun project, and it was great to see how far iOS's standard UI tools could go for games, without needing a full-blown game engine.
