I joined the Stories team in 2022 to work on the camera and capture pipeline. Most of what I shipped is invisible — performance work, memory pressure during long recording sessions, the kind of thing nobody notices until it breaks.

What I'm proudest of is the pre-roll buffer rewrite. We cut frame drops on older devices by about 40%, and I learned more about CMSampleBuffers than I ever wanted to.
