Working for Instagram, in Japan no less, was the opportunity of a lifetime. Instagram is one of those apps that sets the bar for how *nice* an iOS app can look and feel, and getting to contribute to that was an honour.

My team was IG Labs Japan. We were based in Tokyo to research how Japanese users use IG differently from people in the rest of the world.

We worked on a wide range of projects, from IG Maps, to owning the Profile screen, to new features in Stories and Highlights.
