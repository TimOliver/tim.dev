iComics is my main indie project: a DRM-free comic book reader for iPhone and iPad. It first launched in 2012 with a fully skeuomorphic design, and its latest redesign adds support for the iPhone Duo, embracing its new design language and Liquid Glass.

In my (humble) opinion, an app that shows two comic pages side by side is a perfect fit for the iPhone Duo's form factor.

Seeing as iComics already supported side-by-side pages on iPad, I assumed the iPhone Duo would be easy to support. It turns out that between its new vertical navigation buttons and its partially unfolded states, it was a lot more of a challenge than I expected!
