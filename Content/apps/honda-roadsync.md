At Drivemode, an automotive tech startup that Honda acquired just before I joined, I helped build an app for Honda called RoadSync.

RoadSync was made for certain models of Honda motorcycles sold around the world. You pair your phone with your bike, and can then control things like music and navigation far more easily and safely than poking at a touch screen.

Building an app that talked to real hardware was insanely fun, and I learned so much about how Bluetooth devices work.
