This is it: the first iOS app I ever built. It's the app that taught me app development end to end on Apple's new platform, and it was all downhill from there.

Back in the Xbox 360 days, Microsoft didn't list prices on its online store in real money. Everything was priced in its own digital currency, 'Microsoft Points'.

Converting between Microsoft Points and Australian dollars was really confusing. It was something like 80 points to the dollar, so every time I wanted to buy something, I had to do math in my head to work out what it actually cost.

MicroPoints was my solution: a small, quick app that converted any number of Microsoft Points into a range of the world's major currencies.

I sold it for $1, and within a few months it had paid back the cost of my Apple Developer membership. I can't begin to describe how amazing that felt.

A few years later, Microsoft thankfully phased out Points in favour of regular regional pricing, and the app was no longer needed.
