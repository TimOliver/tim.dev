My first real app, and the one that taught me how to ship. iComics was a comic-book reader I built and maintained solo for the better part of a decade, from the original iPad through every redesign Apple threw at us.

It's where I learned about custom UI, image pipelines, and the hard parts of running a one-person App Store business.
