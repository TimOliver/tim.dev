TORoundedButton is a library I built with one goal: to make the most performant, customizable, fluid and reusable rounded button for iOS.

There wasn't an official way to make these buttons until iOS 14. Before that, I kept reinventing the wheel every time I needed one, and the results were usually visually inconsistent or slow.

So in 2019, I set out to build the _best_ rounded button I could, and even now, I'd say it's better than Apple's official offering.

It's built to render as efficiently as possible, which I even got an Apple engineer to confirm for me at WWDC 2019. It also plays a proper, fluid, interruptible animation when tapped.
