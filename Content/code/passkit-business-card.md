This repo makes it easy to create your own business card as a pass in Apple Wallet.

I can't take credit for the idea. I was at an iOS meetup in San Francisco, and when I went to swap cards with another attendee, he showed me his business card in Wallet.

The repo includes a sample pass (with all the JSON and image assets it needs) and a copy of Apple's sample code for signing a pass so it works on real devices.

It's not really a production-ready package, but it's been such a thrill meeting developers, including some at WWDC, who've told me they loved it and shown me the passes they made with it.
