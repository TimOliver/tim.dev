TOCropViewController started life as the cropping engine for iComics. I extracted it into a standalone repo in 2015 and it slowly became the most-starred thing I've ever made.

It's pure UIKit. No SwiftUI bridge, no fancy framework, just a view controller you push or present. That decision is why it still works on iOS 9 through 17 from the same codebase.

It's been used in apps with hundreds of millions of users. Knowing my code is in pockets all over the world is a strange and wonderful feeling.
