BlurUIKit is a collection of UI components and extensions that unlock more dynamic blur effects in UIKit.

The classic example is a progressive Gaussian blur, where the blur radius ramps up along a gradient. It keeps anything overlapping it legible, without needing a hard separator line.

I built it after seeing these effects slowly creep into Apple's own apps in iOS 18, before they took center stage in iOS 26's design language.

I'm still adding more effects. Most recently, I made a blurred mirror component that'll come in handy for certain layouts on the iPhone Duo.
