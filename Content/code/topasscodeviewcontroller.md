TOPasscodeViewController is a library I built in 2017 that recreates the iOS lock screen inside an app.

I made it mainly for iComics, for families sharing an iPad where the parents didn't want their kids getting into the app. It supports light and dark mode, and every device orientation.

Sadly, it's hit a bit of a snag: it looks _too_ much like the real iOS lock screen, and Apple has started rejecting apps that use it over potential security concerns.

When I get some free time, I plan to redesign it away from the standard iOS look to fix that.
