*Welcome to the Cosmos* was an Australian sci-fi series made for the internet, about four ordinary people accidentally swept aboard an alien spaceship mid-mission. A few years earlier I'd volunteered on another project by the director, so when he started shooting in Melbourne he asked me back to help with the CG. I expected to volunteer again, but a little budget turned up in the end, so it became real paid work (not a lot, but it counts!).

I spent a couple of months locked in my bedroom animating 2D and 3D shots for the pilot. This opening sequence was made entirely in After Effects, remixing clips from the episode with a colour tint and the titles. When it was all done, I flew to Melbourne to meet the crew and watch the finished pilot, which went online in 2010.
