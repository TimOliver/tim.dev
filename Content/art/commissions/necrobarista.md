Boss Battle Records, a label run by my friend Fabian Malabello, commissioned and produced the soundtrack for *Necrobarista*. My job was getting the music onto YouTube: I made the videos, adding subtle animation and an animated waveform to the artwork, then exported them all for release.

This one is "Gamble", by Kevin Penkin and Jeremy Lim. Made entirely in Photoshop and After Effects.
