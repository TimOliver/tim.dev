When Apple unveiled iOS 7 in 2013, iComics' extremely custom, skeuomorphic iOS 6 interface broke overnight. Compiled against the new SDK, it was a mess, and Apple soon required apps to ship with it, so there was no updating the app until the whole thing was redesigned.

I rethought every screen from the ground up. Out went the gradients and shadows (mostly), simplifying everything into a cleaner interface with animations that felt at home on iOS 7. It was such a big job that it was closer to a remake, and it's a big part of why iComics v1.1 took so long to ship.
