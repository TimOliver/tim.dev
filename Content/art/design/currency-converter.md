In 2020, in the middle of the pandemic, I suddenly found myself looking for a new job. This was the take-home exercise for one of my interviews: a basic currency converter that pulled live exchange rates from a public API and showed them on screen.

I went all out on the design, even adding iPad and Mac support. I didn't get the job, but I had a really fun time building this app. The design was playful, and it was a great chance to try porting an iOS app to Mac with Mac Catalyst.
