By 2013, my 2008 theme was looking its age, and I felt a bit embarrassed writing about design on a site that was no longer following the latest trends. My posts had also shifted from personal updates to app development news and code techniques, so I strongly felt the blog needed a cleaner, more professional look.

The redesign stripped everything back to a flat, simple layout, focused purely on the blog content. It was my first responsive site, built on Twitter Bootstrap, and it supported everything from full desktop down to iPhone screen sizes. The first MacBook Pro with a Retina display had also recently come out, so I was very proud of making the site support 2x assets so it looked super crisp.
