The look of this blog from 2008 to 2013, back when it ran on WordPress. I forked the base theme and built the whole thing myself.

I designed it in Photoshop first, cut it up into the appropriate assets, and then wrote the HTML and CSS in Sublime Text. It followed the web design trends of the day, with a main content column and a sidebar full of social widgets. The whole site had a red flair (red being my favourite colour), and its glossy buttons matched the style of iPhone OS and Windows Vista at the time.
