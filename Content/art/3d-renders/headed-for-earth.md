In around 2005, a well-known LightWave artist posted a tutorial on rendering a photorealistic Earth: high-quality texture maps, clouds that cast real shadows, and a physically accurate Fresnel rim shading for the atmosphere. This was my attempt at it.

Then I made it more exciting with a motion zoom and a lens flare from the sun, which, admittedly, hid most of the detail.
