Another Star Trek one. Around then, a matte artist from Industrial Light & Magic came to Perth to give a presentation, and I was lucky enough to be in the audience. Among his tips on what makes 3D look realistic, one was an [ambient occlusion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambient_occlusion) pass, which adds soft shading around every bit of detail. I knew of it, but had never really used it in my own work.

So I found an incredibly detailed 3D model of a [Spacedock](https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Spacedock) starbase online, modelled by an artist going by LilPigBoy, and built this vista around it as a test bed. I had also just learned about [OpenEXR](https://openexr.com/), and that LightWave could render several passes into one file. I rendered the scene normally along with a separate ambient occlusion pass, then composited the layers together in Photoshop.

It came out far more detailed than any render I'd done before it.
