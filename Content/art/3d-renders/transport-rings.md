As a hardcore Star Trek fan, I became a huge fan of *Stargate SG-1* too, since it ran in a lot of the same veins. I was thrilled to learn that its visual effects were also made in LightWave.

In the show, an alien race called the Goa'uld travel using these stacks of rings to beam between two locations. I'd made a set of them a few years earlier, but by 2005 my modelling had come a long way, especially the small details like bevels and micro bump maps. They're too subtle to notice on their own, but they add up to a lot more realism.

Using the rings from the show as a rough guide, I designed this version over about three days. The overall shape came from LightWave's lathe, smooth shift and scale tools, and subdivision surfaces then made it high-res. It was modelled, surfaced and rendered in LightWave 8.5.

At the time, I was thinking of making a whole Stargate next, although it felt like everyone was already doing that.
