RPG-X was a mod some friends from around the world and I made for *Star Trek: Voyager – Elite Force*. A group of its players wanted to treat the game as a social roleplaying space instead of a regular deathmatch shooter, so we built one for them.

RPG-X 1.0 was a relatively basic mod that added the core roleplaying features. We followed it with RPG-X 2.0, a much larger overhaul of the entire game.

To drive home how substantial RPG-X 2 was, I took the original 2D logo and made a much more vibrant, detailed 3D version in LightWave. The whole logo was modelled with subdivision surfaces, so its bevels were really detailed and caught the light amazingly.
