Back in 2005, finding 3D models of Star Trek ships that matched the details on TV was pretty hard (in fact, it still is!). An amazing modeller named Sarod made this incredibly show-accurate USS Voyager, and I knew I had to try a render with it.

Voyager is a really interesting ship to light. Its curved hull gives you a lot of freedom to wrap light around it in interesting ways, so this one was mostly lighting practice. It was rendered in LightWave 8 and touched up in Photoshop.
