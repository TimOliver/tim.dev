As well as singing in an anime, I've also sung in an Australian video game!

*Stray Gods: The Roleplaying Musical* is a musical roleplaying game made by Summerfall Studios in Melbourne. It was crowdfunded on Fig, a games-focused alternative to Kickstarter.

The game has an ensemble cast of voice actors and an amazing soundtrack. One of the perks of backing it on Fig was that, if you felt up to it, you could join the background choir on one of the songs. Excited by the opportunity, and keen to support Australian game development, I signed up right away.

I recorded my vocals on my computer in Tokyo and sent the audio files over. The video below starts just before my part comes in.

Please give the game a try! You'll be supporting Australian game development too.
