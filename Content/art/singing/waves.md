As an anime fan since the 90s, it was an honour and a privilege to be part of the choir on the soundtrack of *The Rising of the Shield Hero*, an anime now streaming on Netflix.

How it happened is pretty wild. Kevin Penkin, the composer, and I studied at the same university in Perth, so our paths often crossed in the local Japanese community.

In 2017, he put out a call to his friends for some extra voices for an "upcoming project" he was working on. I was in immediately.

We recorded the vocals together at a studio in Perth, before I moved to Japan. Two years later, the anime premiered on Japanese TV.

If you've watched the show, let me know what you think!
