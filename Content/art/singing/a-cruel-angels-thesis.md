Now hold up! This isn't exactly what it looks like. I was actually working here!

In 2018, I was the English MC for try! Swift, an iOS developer conference in Tokyo. It's a very international conference, with a lot of iOS veterans flying in from America and around the world to speak to Japanese developers through live interpretation.

This was the dinner to welcome the speakers to Japan, held at an event space in Shibuya that had its own karaoke stage and everything.

The Japanese organisers wanted the international speakers to try karaoke, but they knew everyone would be too nervous to go up first. So I was discreetly asked if I could be the one to break the ice.

It worked. After that display, the speakers started lining up for the stage!
